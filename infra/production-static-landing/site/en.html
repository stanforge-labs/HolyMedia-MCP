You control the actions

Changes happen only after approval

HolyPilot reads data by default. When an edit is available, the service shows a preview first and waits for your separate approval.

  • Your company dataAccess is limited to its members and connected accounts.
  • Official connectionYou do not give HolyPilot your advertising platform passwords.
  • Your decisionAvailable edits are not applied without approval.
